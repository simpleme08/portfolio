How I work

Good engineering is a process. I think in systems, not just screens.

01 — DISCOVER

Understand the problem

Clarify users, workflows, constraints and what success actually means.

02 — ARCHITECT

Design for change

Choose pragmatic technologies and structures that keep the product understandable and maintainable.

03 — BUILD

Ship useful software

Build incrementally, validate assumptions and keep the user experience at the center.

04 — SECURE

Protect the product

Authentication, authorization, secrets, data handling and production configuration are part of engineering — not afterthoughts.

05 — DEPLOY

Make it real

Deployment, monitoring, debugging and iteration turn a codebase into a product.
